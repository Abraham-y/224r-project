  THE JUDGE'S AUROC NEVER DEGRADES. First and last checkpoints are
  0.785 [0.757,0.813] and 0.769 [0.737,0.801] -- overlapping.
  Its RANKING survives the whole collapse; the probe's does not.

  But its precision at a frozen threshold falls 0.746 -> 0.429 (-42%),
  against an AUROC change of -2%.
  So AUROC is not a lagging indicator for this monitor -- it is a
  NON-indicator. It would never have warned you at all, at any step,
  while the fraction of flagged rollouts that are actually correct
  nearly halved. That is a stronger form of the paper's claim than
  the probe's 40-step lag, not a weaker one.
